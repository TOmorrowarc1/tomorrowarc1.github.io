I am a third-year computer science undergraduate at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working with Professor Chen Chen.

Several questions I am curious about:

  1. How can we tell whether software behaves as its developers intended, including in unexpected situations?
  2. How can we check whether software conforms to its written specifications, such as API documentation?
  3. How can we identify and address gaps between developers’ intent and the specifications they write?
